# Old Town Hall Historical Accuracy Audit
**Source:** Delta Mill Society, *Old Town Hall Information Brochure* (oth-info-brochure-2025c.pdf)  
**Audit date:** May 2026

---

## Corrections Applied

### 1. Fire time — CRITICAL (multiple locations)
- **Was:** "On the evening of February 24, 1888" / "late in the evening" / "the night air" / "small hours of the night"
- **Now:** "Shortly after noon on Friday, February 24, 1888" / "by about two o'clock in the afternoon" / "by half past four the fire was extinguished"
- **Source:** Brochure quotes the Farmerville (Athens) paper verbatim: fire started "shortly after the hour of noon today" and was extinguished "by half past 4." The fire was an afternoon event, not an evening one. This error appeared in the chapter intro, aside note, act-break section, and timeline entry — all corrected.

### 2. Courthouse/Lodge layout
- **Was:** "a courthouse and lodge upstairs, a meeting place downstairs"
- **Now:** "a Masonic lodge on the second floor, municipal and court offices on the main floor"
- **Source:** Brochure: "The Masonic Lodge occupied the 2nd floor and the main floor served as the Municipal and Court offices and as a courthouse." Lodge is upstairs; courthouse is downstairs.

### 3. Exhibit wall plate descriptions — mislabeled images
- **Was:** Plate VII · 07 = "Bucket line from the creek to the hall" (using oth-1910.jpg, an exterior c.1910 photo)
- **Was:** Plate VIII · 08 = "The Old Town Hall, morning of February 25, 1888" (using oth-play-1900s.jpg, a New Year's play cast photo)
- **Was:** Plate IX · 09 = "An original floor joist, scorched on its hidden face" (using old-town-hall.jpg, a front elevation photo)
- **Now:** All three captions corrected to accurately describe the actual photographs shown.
- **Note:** None of these images document the 1888 fire. Presenting them with false fire-related labels misrepresents the historical record.

### 4. Jail description — embellished detail
- **Was:** "A small jail was set into the basement — a single iron-fitted room whose door, removed long ago, is recalled in oral histories as remarkably heavy for so modest a chamber."
- **Now:** "A small jail occupied the basement."
- **Source:** Brochure only says "(with a jail in the basement)." The "iron-fitted room," "heavy door," and "oral histories" detail is not in the source.

### 5. Brick-making description — embellished
- **Was:** "then laid out in long rows to dry under the summer sun before firing"
- **Now:** "stacked and allowed to dry" / "stacked to dry"
- **Source:** Brochure: "The clay was pressed into moulds by hand and then stacked and allowed to dry." The "long rows," "summer sun," and "before firing" details are not in the source.

### 6. Brick-making process step iv — speculative kiln detail
- **Was:** "Wood-fired in the yard's small kiln, then carted to the building site and laid into the walls"
- **Now:** "Carted to the building site on King Street and laid into the walls of the new town hall"
- **Source:** The brochure does not mention a kiln or firing process. Removed.

### 7. Jasper Russell brickyard location
- **Was:** "near the site of today's post office"
- **Now:** "behind today's post office"
- **Source:** Brochure: "located close to the Old Town Hall in an area behind today's Post Office."

### 8. Jasper Russell price — verifiable detail added
- **Added:** "Russell charged four cents per thousand bricks."
- **Source:** Brochure: "Russell charged 4 cents per 1,000 bricks."

### 9. Vault date
- **Was:** "c. 1910 · Vault"
- **Now:** "Early 1900s · Vault"
- **Source:** The brochure says the vault was built alongside the side addition in the early 1900s. No specific year is given.

### 10. Fire source attribution
- **Was:** "the village's weekly newspaper" / "DMS Archive · Box 4 · Folder ii"
- **Now:** "Farmerville (Athens) paper"
- **Source:** The brochure identifies the source as "the Farmerville (Athens) paper." Athens is a separate village. "DMS Archive · Box 4 · Folder ii" is an invented archival citation with no basis in the source.

### 11. Invented source references — removed
- **Removed:** "reconstructed from the contemporary press, the township minute book of the following March, and the recollections preserved in the Society's oral history collection"
- **Source:** The brochure references only the contemporary press account. No "township minute book" or "oral history collection" is cited.

### 12. Object label accession number — removed
- **Was:** "Object IX · DMS Collection · Acc. 2012-118"
- **Was:** "the charred surface, hidden for 124 years, is now on permanent display within the building"
- **Now:** Simplified to: "Confirmed during the 2012 restoration · DMS"
- **Source:** Brochure says charred wood remains hidden behind walls and under floors — not on "permanent display." The accession number "Acc. 2012-118" is invented.

### 13. "Theatre" in opening subtitle — removed
- **Was:** "A gathering place, courthouse, theatre, lodge..."
- **Now:** "A gathering place, courthouse, Masonic lodge, and community hall"
- **Source:** Brochure does not describe the hall as a theatre. It mentions a New Year's Day play as a community event, but not a theatre function.

### 14. Hero area: "Designated Heritage Property · Township of Rideau Lakes · 1879"
- **Was:** "Designated Heritage Property / Township of Rideau Lakes · 1879"
- **Now:** "Built 1879 – 80 / Township of Bastard & South Burgess"
- **Source:** (1) The brochure does not mention a formal heritage designation. (2) Township of Rideau Lakes was formed in 2001 — it did not exist in 1879. The original township was "Bastard & South Burgess." (3) Labelling "1879" as a designation date is misleading — it is the construction date.

### 15. Delta Lion's Club — missing detail added
- **Added:** "The building was then used for a time by the Delta Lion's Club."
- **Source:** Brochure: "In 1979, the municipality and lodge vacated the building. It was then used for a time by the Delta Lion's Club."

### 16. Museum period — specific dates added
- **Was:** "the hall later served as a museum" / "2000s"
- **Now:** "From 1999 to 2011, the Society used the main hall as a museum" / timeline entry updated to "1999 – 2011"
- **Source:** Brochure: "The DMS used the main hall as a museum from 1999 to 2011."
